# Wisdom Duel digital rules

Two players each choose three **Creatures** and start with **20 Power**. The three Creatures share their player's Power. Reduce your opponent to 0 Power to win. If both players reach 0 or less simultaneously, the duel is a draw.

## Cards

A **Creature** has an element, a passive ability and a **Wisdom** cycle. Rotating a Creature advances to the next value printed on its cycle, up to the fourth position. Tarasca, for example, goes from 0 Wisdom to 2 on its first rotation.

A **Knowledge card** is either an **Ally** or a **Spell**. The Market starts with five face-up Knowledge cards from the shared deck. Your hand holds the cards you have drawn. Each Creature can hold one Knowledge card in play.

| Element | Allies | Spells |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air | Lepidoptera, Owl, Chiropter | Blue Sky, Migration |
| Water | Asteroid, Delphinidae, Galapago | Tsunami, Hurricane |
| Earth | Ursus, Serpent, Lupus | Earthquake, Fire |

Creature elements also include Fire. The card named *Fire* is an Earth Spell.

## Knowledge Phase

At the start of your turn, each of **your** Knowledge cards in play resolves its effect at its current rotation and then advances one step. After the final step it leaves play and goes to the discard pile. Some effects ask you to choose a target. Cards on the other side of the table can still provide Defense or a passive effect.

**Damage** reduces the opposing player's Power after applicable **Defense**. For example, 2 damage against 1 Defense normally costs the opponent 1 Power. Gaining Power increases your team's total.

## Action Phase

You normally have two actions.

- **Draw from the Market.** Take one face-up Knowledge card into your hand. The Market refills from the deck when possible. If your hand exceeds five cards, choose what to discard.
- **Rotate a Creature.** Advance one of your Creatures one step on its Wisdom cycle.
- **Play a Knowledge card.** Choose an Ally or a Spell from your hand, then a Creature that meets its Wisdom requirement. Playing a card that requires 1 Wisdom on a Creature with 2 Wisdom leaves the Creature with 2 Wisdom. Playing normally uses one action. Some passives make it free.
- If the chosen Creature already holds a Knowledge card, the old card leaves play and is discarded. Hurricane can block play on the Creature facing it.

Where the board offers **Rotate a Knowledge card**, you can spend an action to advance an attached card one step. This can take it out of play.

Choose **End Turn** to finish early. Spending your last action normally ends the turn after pending choices resolve.

## Reading effects

| Wording | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| **On play** | When the card enters play from your hand. Lepidoptera gives its player 1 Power immediately. |
| **While in play** | A continuous effect. Owl gives your Creatures +1 Wisdom while it is on your board. |
| **During your Knowledge Phase** | At the card's current rotation on your turn. Fire discards every opposing Knowledge card in play requiring 2 Wisdom or less, on either rotation. |
| **On final rotation** | When the card resolves the last step of its cycle in your Knowledge Phase. Galapago grants an extra action. |
| **When this card leaves play** | On any departure from the board, including replacement. Lisovik reacts when one of your Earth Knowledge cards leaves play. |

The [card compendium](https://wisdomduel.mythicalbeings.io/how-to-play) shows each card's subtype, Wisdom requirement, effect and cycle. The [physical rulebook](../../public/RULEBOOK.pdf) documents the printed edition. The printed Fire targets Allies. In Wisdom Duel it can discard both Allies and Spells.
